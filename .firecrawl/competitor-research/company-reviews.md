## Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to frequently asked questions about REVIEWS.io to help you better understand our features, services, and how we can support your needs.

Why collect company reviews in addition to product reviews?

Company reviews build overall trust in your brand, especially early in the buyer journey. They show potential customers you’re reliable as a business, not just at the product level. Together with [product reviews](https://www.reviews.io/front/productreviews), they give shoppers a complete and credible view of your reputation.

Can I use incentives to collect company reviews?

No. Global regulations prohibit offering rewards for company reviews, as all feedback must remain unbiased and authentic. As a Google Licensed Partner, REVIEWS.io validates every review submitted through our platform, so the reviews you collect - and the ones we share with Google - are genuine, trustworthy, and fully compliant.

How do company reviews impact Google Seller Ratings?

To qualify for Google Seller Ratings, you need at least 100 verified company reviews in the target country, collected within the past 24 months, with an average rating of 3.5 stars or higher.

[‍](https://www.reviews.io/front/attributes)

Once these requirements are met, Google may automatically display your Seller Rating in ads and search results - boosting click-through rates by 10–17%.

‍

Where can I collect company reviews from?

You can invite customers to leave company reviews via email, SMS, in-store touchpoints, or directly on your website. With automated flows, you can time invites perfectly, whether online or offline, to maximise response rates.

Are SMS review invites really more effective?

Yes. SMS review invites can achieve up to 3x higher response rates than email alone. By combining both channels in automated flows, you can reach customers in the way they’re most likely to engage.

Can I manage reviews from other platforms too?

Yes. With our [Reputation Manager](https://www.reviews.io/front/reputation-management), you can monitor and respond to reviews across Google, Facebook, Trustpilot, and other third-party platforms. This gives you one central dashboard to manage your full brand reputation.

How do I display company reviews on my site?

You can showcase company reviews using customizable widgets across your homepage, landing pages, or even within email campaigns. This boosts credibility at every touchpoint and helps convert new visitors into customers.
