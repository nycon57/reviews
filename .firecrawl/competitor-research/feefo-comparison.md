## Pricing Model Summary

- **Invite-based pricing**: pay per review invitation sent (email or SMS), not fixed contracts
- **Month-to-month plans**: no annual lock-in, upgrade/downgrade anytime
- **All core features included**: no hidden add-ons for integrations, analytics, or contract renewals
- **Contract buyout program**: will honor remainder of existing Feefo contract
- **Data ownership**: full export/portability of collected reviews

**Feefo pricing comparison claims (by REVIEWS.io):**
- Feefo uses annual contracts with rigid renewal terms
- Feefo charges separately for advanced features and integrations
- Feefo's pricing requires a sales call (not transparent)

## Go-to-Market Positioning

**Core differentiation claims against Feefo:**
1. **Open ecosystem vs closed network** — Feefo keeps reviews within its own network; REVIEWS.io syndicates across 25+ platforms
2. **Transparent pricing** — invite-based, month-to-month vs Feefo's annual contracts
3. **AI-powered features** — AI Summary, Reply, Insights not available in Feefo
4. **Built-in loyalty** — rewards and repeat purchase programs vs feedback-only
5. **Migration ease** — 3-step switch process (export CSV, send to team, go live in days)
6. **Integration breadth** — native connections to Klaviyo, Gorgias, Shopify etc. vs Feefo's more limited ecosystem

**Migration pitch:** 3-step process — export from Feefo, send CSV, REVIEWS.io imports and matches templates. Contract buyout available.

**URL pattern:** `?switching_from=feefo` parameter passed to demo booking for sales attribution.
