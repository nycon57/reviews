## Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to frequently asked questions about REVIEWS.io to help you better understand our features, services, and how we can support your needs.

How is Invitation Flow different from standard review invite delays?

While the review template lets you set a basic delay, Invitation Flow gives you full control to build personalised journeys. You can tailor invites based on factors like location, shipping timelines, or product type - for example, delaying invites to Australia if orders ship from the UK. It’s designed for brands that need more flexibility than a one-size-fits-all delay.

Can I customize when and how review invites are sent?

Yes. You can control the timing, frequency, and conditions - like delaying invites by location, sending different templates based on product type, or following up only if a review hasn’t been submitted.

Can I trigger different Flows for different types of customers?

Absolutely. Use filters like purchase history, location, product collection, or social engagement to tailor who gets which message and when.

What happens if someone ignores the first review invite?

You can set up follow-up emails to automatically remind customers who didn’t respond the first time, improving your overall collection rate without lifting a finger.

Can I collect UGC using Flows?

Yes. You can set rules to ask only your 5-star reviewers for a photo or video after they leave a review - making it easy to collect high-quality UGC from happy customers who are most likely to share.

How do Flows help with customer retention?

Flows help you respond at the right time, like checking in after a low rating or following up after a positive one with personalised outreach. That kind of responsiveness builds trust and keeps customers coming back.
