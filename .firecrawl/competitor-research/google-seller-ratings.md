## Frequently asked questions

Find answers to frequently asked questions about REVIEWS.io to help you better understand our features, services, and how we can support your needs.

Is REVIEWS.io a Google Licensed Partner?

Yes. REVIEWS.io is an official Google review partner, approved to collect and share verified reviews for Google Seller Ratings and Rich Snippets. We sync review data with Google daily for faster activation.

What are the requirements for Google Seller Ratings?

To qualify, you need at least 100 verified reviews per country collected within the past 24 months, and an average rating of 3.5 stars or higher.

How long does it take for my Google Seller Ratings to appear?

Once you meet the requirements, it typically takes 1–4 weeks for reviews to appear across Google Search, Shopping, and YouTube ads. REVIEWS.io shares data with Google every 24 hours to keep your ratings current.

How can I track my eligibility for Google Seller Ratings?

Your REVIEWS.io dashboard includes an "Eligible for Seller Ratings" section where you can see how many reviews you have and how close you are to reaching the threshold.

What's the difference between Google Seller Ratings and Review Snippets?

Seller Ratings appear on paid ads (Google Search, Shopping, YouTube) and require verified reviews.

Review Snippets appear in organic search results, showing star ratings and excerpts pulled from product or company pages with structured data markup.

How do Google stars help my ads perform better?

Google Seller Ratings can boost ad click-through rates by up to 17%, helping you capture more traffic, lower cost-per-click, and improve overall ROAS by adding instant trust to your paid listings.

Why choose an official Google review partner over manual integration?

Official partners like REVIEWS.io follow Google's technical guidelines, meaning reviews sync faster, data is more accurate, and there's no need to wait months or rely on unsupported workarounds.

Which REVIEWS.io plans include Google integrations?

Google integrations - like Rich Snippets, Shopping stars, and Seller Ratings - are included on the Start-Up plan and above.
