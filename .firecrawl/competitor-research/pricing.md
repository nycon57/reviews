## Frequently asked questions

Find answers to frequently asked questions about REVIEWS.io to help you better understand our features, services, and how we can support your needs.

How quickly can I start collecting verified reviews?

Right away. Once you connect your store, you can send invites to new buyers instantly. You can also use Review Booster to reach out to past customers, so you don’t start from zero. Most brands see verified reviews rolling in within 24 hours and are fully live in under a week.

What counts as a “review invite”?

Each email or SMS request sent via REVIEWS.io counts as one invite. If you send via external tools (like Klaviyo), it’s only counted once the recipient clicks through to the Review Collector Page. Each invite counts once—no hidden charges or double-counting.

Who owns the reviews and UGC collected?

You do. You retain full ownership of all review and UGC content, with the ability to export at any time. Unlike some platforms that restrict exports, suppress reviews, or hold content hostage, REVIEWS.io gives you full control—no lock-ins, no suppression, ever.

Can I import my existing reviews?

Yes. REVIEWS.io makes it easy to import reviews from platforms like Yotpo, Okendo, or Trustpilot. All review data—including ratings, timestamps, and authorship—is preserved, so your SEO and Google Seller Ratings remain intact during migration.

Does REVIEWS.io support multiple domains or stores?

Yes. You can manage multiple stores under one REVIEWS.io account. Reviews can be collected centrally and then displayed locally (e.g., US, UK, AU), ensuring each storefront shows authentic, relevant reviews without siloed setups.

Do I need to sign an annual contract?

No. All plans are month-to-month with transparent invite-based pricing. You’re never locked in. If you prefer to commit annually, we also offer a 20% discount compared to monthly billing.
