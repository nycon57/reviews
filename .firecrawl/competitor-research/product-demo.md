**Essentials**

## Invites & publishing

Simplicity is key when sending an email invitation for both products & company reviews. Keeping it short is a proven strategy, leaving them with a single focus... leave a review.

Be genuine and personal with a sincere thank you

Make the request clear and direct with a single call to action

Provide a simple link and express gratitude for their feedback
