## Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to frequently asked questions about REVIEWS.io to help you better understand our features, services, and how we can support your needs.

How can I measure the conversion impact of my product reviews?

REVIEWS.io offers built-in analytics to track how reviews influence on-site behaviour, including click-throughs, conversions, and time on page. You can also run A/B tests or connect to tools like GA4 to monitor uplift in performance tied to reviews.

Can I collect photo or video reviews from customers?

Yes! You can [request visual reviews](https://www.reviews.io/front/ugc) through post-purchase emails, QR codes, or direct links. These bring your products to life and boost engagement across product pages, emails, and ads.

What are Review Attributes, and why should I use them?

[Review Attributes](https://www.reviews.io/front/attributes) let you collect structured data - like size fit, skin type, or usage frequency - alongside each review. They help shoppers filter reviews and give you better insight into product performance.

How do product reviews improve ad performance?

Verified product reviews power Google Shopping stars and Review Snippets. They add social proof to your ads, increase click-through rates, and reduce acquisition costs by building trust early.

Can product reviews help reduce support tickets?

Yes. Attributes and review content help answer common questions about sizing, quality, and use - so customers get the info they need without reaching out to support.

Do REVIEWS.io product reviews qualify for Google Shopping stars?

Yes, as long as they meet Google’s requirements. Verified reviews collected via approved methods can [appear in Google Shopping ads](https://www.reviews.io/front/google-seller-ratings) and product listings.
