## Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to frequently asked questions about REVIEWS.io to help you better understand our features, services, and how we can support your needs.

How can I collect more reviews without spamming my customers?

Our automated review invites are designed to respect your customers’ time. You control when and how invites are sent, so you can maintain a personal, non-intrusive approach while still driving more responses.

Can I import my reviews and keep my Google stars if I switch platforms?

Yes. You can import existing reviews from platforms like Trustpilot, Yotpo, and Judge.me. We’ll help ensure they’re formatted correctly and eligible for Google Seller Ratings, so you retain your star visibility and search performance.

What’s the best way to ask for reviews from past customers?

Use our Review Booster to quickly reach out to previous customers in bulk. It’s ideal for brands starting fresh or [switching platforms](https://www.reviews.io/front/switch-provider) and helps you generate immediate social proof.

Do I need both product and company reviews?

Both serve different purposes. [Product reviews](https://www.reviews.io/front/productreviews) influence buying decisions at the item level, while [company reviews](https://www.reviews.io/front/company-reviews) build brand trust. Together, they give shoppers a fuller picture of your reputation.

Are incentivised reviews allowed?

You can offer incentives for product reviews, [as long as the reward is clearly disclosed](https://updates.reviews.io/mark-reviews-as-incentivized) and not conditional on a positive rating. However, company reviews must remain completely organic to comply with Google’s policies, and we recommend consulting your legal team to ensure your approach aligns with local regulations.

Do I own the content collected through REVIEWS.io?

Yes. All review content collected through our platform is yours to use across your site, marketing campaigns, ads, and more. Unlike some platforms, REVIEWS.io gives you full ownership and control over your hard-earned customer feedback - whether you’re starting fresh or switching from another provider.
