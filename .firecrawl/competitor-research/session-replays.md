## Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to frequently asked questions about REVIEWS.io to help you better understand our features, services, and how we can support your needs.

How can AI Assistant actually improve my review strategy?

The AI Assistant instantly scans your review and attribute data to surface trends, pain points, and product insights - so you can spot issues early, highlight what’s working, and make smarter decisions without hours of manual analysis.

What kind of questions can I ask the AI Assistant?

You can ask things like:

• “Why are people returning this product?”

• “What’s driving 5-star reviews for \[product\]?”

• “Which delivery issues come up most often?”

The AI Assistant analyses your review content and customer feedback to give you instant, practical insights. You can type your own question, or use one of the built-in prompts to get started.

Will the AI replies sound robotic or off-brand?

No. Replies are tailored to each review and you’re always in control. You can edit or personalise them before sending - or just use them as a helpful starting point.

Can the AI help me improve specific products?

Yes. The tools can surface feature-specific praise and criticism, so you know what’s working and what needs fixing - ideal for product development or pre-launch analysis.

How is this different from just reading reviews myself?

AI catches patterns you might miss - especially when you’ve got hundreds or thousands of reviews. It filters noise, spots trends, and gives you fast, data-backed direction without the overwhelm.

Who benefits most from using a combination of AI tools?

If you're scaling fast, launching new products, or trying to improve retention, AI gives your team the clarity and time they need to stay focused and make better calls.

Is the AI Assistant trained on my reviews and data?

The AI Assistant analyses the verified reviews and Attribute data you collect through REVIEWS.io. That means the insights are based entirely on your own customer feedback - so the answers are specific to your brand, not generic AI responses.
