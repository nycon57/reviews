# Competitor Analysis: REVIEWS.io Survey Capabilities

> Scraped on: 2025-01-31
> Source: https://www.reviews.io/front/surveys

## Overview

REVIEWS.io offers a survey module positioned as a customer insights tool with high response rates. It supports multiple survey deployment methods and over 50 templates with 8 question types.

## Key Capabilities

- **On-site surveys**: Triggered at pivotal moments, segmented by collection/product URL
- **Post-checkout surveys**: Launched after purchase for attribution, product quality, delivery feedback
- **Shareable link surveys**: Distributed via email, SMS, or social for high-volume feedback campaigns
- **Post-review surveys**: Follow-up surveys triggered by review score (e.g., ask 3-star reviewers what held them back)
- **50+ templates**: Pre-designed templates with customizable builder and styling
- **8 question types**: Multiple format options for different data collection needs
- **NPS tracking**: Built-in Net Promoter Score measurement
- **Klaviyo/CRM integration**: Survey responses tied to customer profiles for segmentation

## Strategic Gaps & Opportunities

1. **Attribution surveys are a differentiator** — REVIEWS.io emphasizes post-purchase attribution ("How did you find us?") as a replacement for pixel tracking. RepWell should consider this for mortgage/financial services where tracking is harder.

2. **Score-based follow-up surveys** — Triggering different surveys based on review rating is a strong retention tool. We should build this into our review workflow.

3. **On-site survey placement** — REVIEWS.io places surveys at URL-specific moments. For financial services, this could map to post-application, post-closing, or refinance consideration stages.

4. **Template library depth** — 50+ templates is a significant head start. RepWell should prioritize mortgage/financial-specific templates rather than competing on volume.

5. **Cart abandonment surveys** — Less relevant for financial services, but the concept of "friction point identification surveys" applies to loan application drop-off.
