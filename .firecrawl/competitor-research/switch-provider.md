## Switch-proof Questions.

We understand that moving review platforms can be a big deal. That’s why we’ve made the process as easy as possible for you and why we preempt any concerns you have below:

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/642419dd2be643c50e400f66/64b94819c538bc07852004b9_ProductReviews_EasiestSetup_Mid-Market_EaseOfSetup.svg)

Will I lose my existing reviews?

Rest assured, when migrating your reviews, you won't lose them. You can maintain ownership of your review content by importing them through a CSV file. If your previous provider was a Google-licensed partner, your reviews will remain visible during the switch. The migration process typically takes just one day, ensuring your reviews are published promptly.

How simple is the Switch process?

It's incredibly straightforward. REVIEWS.io has been built with the end user in mind. It's a quick setup, easy to navigate, and uses a code-free approach across all features. Simply import your existing reviews from your existing review provider.

Can the solution grow with my business?

Yes. Our flexible tier structure and monthly subscription offer the flexibility to adjust your plan based on your business needs. Whether you need to upgrade or downgrade, we provide the means to support your business's growth through our range of available tiers.

What level of customization does REVIEWS.io offer?

Our super-fast widgets can be customized within the widget editor. This allows clients to align their review content with their brand's unique look and feel, ensuring a seamless and intuitive implementation of our widgets that perfectly suits their brand.
