## Let’s set the record straight

While others aim to be the biggest, we aim to be the best. Your success drives every decision we make, and we continually refine our product to ensure you get a top-tier solution at a fair cost.

Is Trustpilot a better value?

The annual deals you’re tied into are restrictive. We offer far greater flexibility with cost-effective monthly plans - no hidden fees, no price hikes. And with our buy-out deal, you can try us risk-free.

James from Abbott Lyon says, “Brands join Trustpilot for all the wrong reasons - it’s about damage limitation, not about creating good content around your product or service”.

We know customers can be sceptical of REVIEWS.io. We may not be the first brand you think of - and we’re okay with that - because we’ll be the last one you’ll ever need to switch to.

Is REVIEWS.io a Google Licensed Partner?

Yes! REVIEWS.io is an official Google partner, helping you collect and share verified reviews for Google Seller Ratings and Rich Snippets. We sync your review data daily so your ratings appear faster on Google.

Is Trustpilot helping us rank better?

No, Trustpilot itself doesn’t help you rank better - your content does. Having review stars displayed on your Google Ads and product listings in Google Shopping improves visibility and increases CTR, which in turn can lower your PPC costs.

Like Trustpilot, REVIEWS.io is a Google Licensed Reviews Partner and can ensure that you qualify for Google Seller Ratings. Just because one review platform is more well-known than another doesn’t mean Google automatically prioritizes its content.

Trustpilot often tries to leverage this misconception in their proposals - but that’s simply not true.

Do I own my Trustpilot content, such as reviews and user-generated content (UGC)?

Unfortunately, Trustpilot retains the publishing rights to all the content you’ve gathered through its platform. This means you can’t freely use that content, as the ownership belongs to Trustpilot.

However, don’t worry - we’ve developed a process that allows you to preserve this valuable content when you decide to switch. The best part? We transfer the publishing rights to you. So, if you ever choose to stop working with us, you can take your content with you, anytime.

In contrast to Trustpilot, which retains ownership of review data, REVIEWS.io ensures you maintain full control over your reviews and UGC.
