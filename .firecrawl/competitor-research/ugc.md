# Competitor Analysis: UGC Features (REVIEWS.io)

> Scraped on: 2025-01-31
> Source: https://www.reviews.io/front/ugc

## Observed Capabilities

- **Photo & video review collection**: Automated post-purchase requests for photo/video reviews
- **Instagram/tagged content aggregation**: Pull tagged social posts from customers into a unified gallery
- **Shoppable UGC galleries**: Tag products in any customer image to create clickable, purchasable galleries
- **Klaviyo email integration**: Embed dynamic UGC content blocks in Klaviyo templates (abandoned cart, recommendations); claims up to 78% click-through rate boost
- **QR code & SMS collection**: Offline-to-online UGC capture methods
- **Content rights management**: Built-in tools to request and track customer content usage permissions
- **Brand/category galleries**: UGC galleries organized by product or brand category
- **No-code gallery builder**: Drag-and-drop creation of shoppable galleries without design or dev skills

## Roadmap Considerations for RepWell

1. **Video testimonial UGC** — We already have video testimonial features. Consider adding shoppable or embeddable gallery views for loan officer profiles.
2. **Social content aggregation** — Pulling tagged social mentions into a unified dashboard could strengthen our social publishing module.
3. **Email UGC embedding** — Integrating testimonial snippets or review photos into Resend email templates could boost engagement.
4. **Rights management** — Important for compliance in financial services; build permission tracking into our testimonial workflow.
5. **QR code collection** — Useful for branch/office locations to collect in-person testimonials.
