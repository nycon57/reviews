# Competitor Analysis: REVIEWS.io Video Reviews

> Analysis date: 2025-01-31
> Source URLs: https://www.reviews.io/front/video-reviews, https://www.reviews.io/front/video-first
> Confidence level: Medium (video-reviews page returned 404; analysis based on video-first page and site navigation)

## Feature Comparison

| Capability | REVIEWS.io | RepWell (Current/Planned) |
|------------|-----------|---------------------------|
| Video review collection | Yes — email/SMS invite campaigns | Yes — video testimonial requests |
| Video-first invites | Yes — dedicated video invite templates | Planned |
| Auto-collect from happy customers | Yes — triggered by positive reviews | Planned |
| Pre-designed invite templates | Yes — multiple talent/brand variants | TBD |
| On-site video widget | Yes — embeddable player | Yes — video player section |
| Social media publishing | Yes — share to social from dashboard | Yes — social publishing actions |
| Video UGC galleries | Yes — shoppable video galleries | Partial |
| Platform integrations | Shopify, BigCommerce, WooCommerce, Klaviyo, HubSpot, Attentive | Supabase-native, Resend |

## Key Observations

- REVIEWS.io emphasizes "video-first" as a distinct campaign type, separate from text review invites
- They provide pre-built video invite templates featuring diverse talent for different brand audiences
- Video reviews are positioned as boosting conversions by up to 20%
- Auto-collection from happy customers reduces manual effort in sourcing video content
- The original `/front/video-reviews` URL returned a 404, suggesting this feature may have been consolidated under `/front/video-first`

## Gaps & Opportunities

1. **Video-first campaign mode** — RepWell should offer a dedicated "request video" flow separate from standard review requests
2. **Template library for video invites** — Pre-built email/SMS templates specifically designed to encourage video submissions
3. **Conversion metrics** — Track and display how video testimonials impact loan officer conversion rates
4. **Auto-trigger on positive reviews** — When a customer leaves a 5-star review, automatically prompt for a video testimonial follow-up

## Recommended Actions

- Audit our current video testimonial request flow against REVIEWS.io's video-first approach
- Consider adding video-specific invite templates to our email/SMS distribution
- Build reporting on video testimonial impact (views, engagement, conversion attribution)
