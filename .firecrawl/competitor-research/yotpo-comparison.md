## Frequently asked questions

Find answers to frequently asked questions about REVIEWS.io to help you better understand our features, services, and how we can support your needs.

What eCommerce platforms can I use with REVIEWS.io?

REVIEWS.io offers direct integrations with Shopify, Shopify Plus, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Magento, and PrestaShop. For those on custom stores or headless setups, a robust and well-documented API is available to customers on our Plus plan. We’re proud to offer the same great features and support regardless of the platform you’re selling on.

Is REVIEWS.io a Google review Partner?

Yes, REVIEWS.io has been an official Google Review Partner for more than ten years.

What counts as a "review invite"?

Each email or SMS request sent through the REVIEWS.io platform counts as a review invite. For invites sent outside of the REVIEWS.io platform (e.g. via Klaviyo), a review invite is counted when the recipient clicks the review link and reaches the Review Collector Page.

I’m stuck in an annual contract. Do you offer contract buyouts?

A contract buyout may be available. Book a demo with our team to learn more about your contract buyout options.

Can I customize the look and feel of my review widgets?

Yes. All REVIEWS.io widgets are fully customizable. You can adjust colors, fonts, layouts, and styling through the built-in widget editor to match your brand's look and feel. CSS overrides are supported for advanced customization, and widget configuration options include layout variants (grid, carousel, list), display density, and responsive breakpoints.

Who owns the reviews collected using REVIEWS.io?

You remain in full ownership of the reviews and UGC you collect using our platform. Simply export them when needed.
