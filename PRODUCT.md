# Product

## Register

product

## Users

RepWell serves individual professionals and enterprise teams in sales-based industries, with mortgage and financial services as the current strongest fit. Individual users need to collect reviews, build public credibility, respond to customers, and turn positive feedback into reputation growth. Managers and administrators need organization-level control: approvals, team performance, review routing, analytics, compliance-aware publishing, integrations, and clear evidence that the system is working.

Customers also touch the product through public survey, review, video testimonial, verification, smart link, and preference flows. Those surfaces must feel trustworthy, low-friction, and respectful because they directly affect review quality and conversion.

## Product Purpose

RepWell replaces generic reputation platforms such as Experience.com and Birdeye with a review, survey, video testimonial, analytics, and reputation workflow built for high-trust service businesses. It consolidates first-party feedback, external reviews, AI analysis, review response workflows, share-ready assets, profile pages, widgets, and team oversight into one product.

Success means professionals collect more usable reviews, managers can govern the process without slowing people down, customers understand what is being asked of them, and the business can safely amplify positive reputation signals across public profiles, owned pages, widgets, emails, and social assets.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy. Intelligent. Warm.

The product voice is professional, confident, and approachable. It should feel premium without being sterile, expert without sounding corporate-stiff, and helpful without becoming cute. The interface should leave users feeling in control of their reputation and clear on the next action.

## Anti-references

Avoid cluttered dashboards, aggressive conversion patterns, sterile corporate SaaS, overly playful startup aesthetics, trend-chasing AI visuals, generic gray admin panels, and mortgage-only language in reusable UI. Avoid interfaces that hide approval state, bury customer context, or make publishing and response workflows feel casual when they need traceability.

Do not drift into dark blue enterprise sameness, neon AI tooling, purple gradient SaaS, glassmorphism, decorative motion, or crowded review-management dashboards that make every metric feel equally urgent.

## Design Principles

1. **Confidence before decoration.** Make approval status, publishing state, source, ownership, and next action obvious before adding visual personality.
2. **Warm professionalism.** Use the brand's teal and sage system, editorial display moments, and generous whitespace to build trust without softening the product into lifestyle marketing.
3. **Progressive disclosure.** Show the immediate task first, then reveal advanced filters, AI detail, compliance context, and admin controls when the workflow calls for them.
4. **Industry-agnostic by default.** UI copy should serve sales-based professionals broadly; mortgage-specific language belongs only where the workflow explicitly requires it.
5. **Traceable automation.** AI suggestions, review routing, publishing, video processing, and scheduled follow-ups need visible state, recoverable actions, and human-readable outcomes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Design and implementation should target WCAG 2.1 AA. Text contrast must be checked on teal and sage surfaces, focus states must be visible, keyboard navigation must work across dialogs, dropdowns, tables, review queues, and media controls, and motion must respect reduced-motion preferences. Public customer flows should be readable, mobile-first, and forgiving of incomplete inputs, media permission failures, and low-bandwidth recording conditions.
