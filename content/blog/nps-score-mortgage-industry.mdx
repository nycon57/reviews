# Understanding NPS: What's a Good Score for the Mortgage Industry?

Net Promoter Score (NPS) has become the gold standard for measuring customer loyalty across industries. But what does a "good" NPS look like specifically for mortgage professionals? Let's dive in.

## What is NPS?

NPS measures how likely your customers are to recommend your services to others. It's based on a single question:

> "On a scale of 0-10, how likely are you to recommend [Company/LO Name] to a friend or colleague?"

### How It's Calculated

Respondents are grouped into three categories:

- **Promoters (9-10)**: Loyal enthusiasts who will refer others
- **Passives (7-8)**: Satisfied but unenthusiastic
- **Detractors (0-6)**: Unhappy customers who can damage your brand

```
NPS = % Promoters - % Detractors
```

The score ranges from -100 to +100.

## NPS Benchmarks by Industry

| Industry | Average NPS |
|----------|-------------|
| Retail Banking | 35 |
| Insurance | 30 |
| **Mortgage Lending** | **28** |
| Credit Unions | 50 |
| Fintech | 45 |

## What's a Good NPS for Mortgage Professionals?

Based on industry data:

- **Below 0**: Needs immediate attention
- **0-30**: Room for improvement
- **30-50**: Good - above industry average
- **50-70**: Excellent - strong customer loyalty
- **70+**: World-class - exceptional performance

### Top Performers

The best mortgage companies and loan officers typically achieve NPS scores of **60+**. What sets them apart?

1. **Proactive communication** throughout the loan process
2. **Setting realistic expectations** early
3. **Quick response times** to questions and concerns
4. **Post-close follow-up** and relationship building
5. **Systematic review collection** at optimal moments

## Improving Your NPS

### Focus on Detractors

Every detractor tells you something valuable:

- What went wrong?
- Was it within your control?
- How can you prevent it?

### Convert Passives to Promoters

Passives are your biggest opportunity:

- Follow up personally after closing
- Ask what would have made their experience a 10
- Offer value beyond the transaction (market updates, refinance alerts)

### Celebrate Promoters

Don't take promoters for granted:

- Thank them personally for their feedback
- Ask for referrals when appropriate
- Feature their testimonials (with permission)

## Tracking NPS Over Time

NPS is most valuable as a trend metric:

```
Month 1: NPS 32
Month 2: NPS 35
Month 3: NPS 41
Month 4: NPS 45
```

Consistent improvement indicates your customer experience initiatives are working.

## Beyond the Score

While NPS is valuable, combine it with:

- **CSAT** (Customer Satisfaction Score) for transactional feedback
- **CES** (Customer Effort Score) for process friction
- **Qualitative feedback** from open-ended questions

## Conclusion

A good NPS for mortgage professionals is **above 30**, but the best aim for **50+**. Use NPS as a north star metric, but don't forget to dig into the "why" behind the numbers.
