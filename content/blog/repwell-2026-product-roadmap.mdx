# RepWell 2026: What's Coming This Year

Happy New Year! As we kick off 2026, I wanted to share our product vision and the exciting features we're bringing to RepWell this year. Our mission remains the same: help mortgage professionals collect more reviews, understand their customers better, and grow their business.

## Q1: AI & Analytics Enhancements

### AI Insights Dashboard

We're launching a comprehensive AI-powered insights dashboard that will:

- **Sentiment trends** - Track how customer feelings change over time
- **Theme analysis** - Automatically categorize feedback topics
- **Competitive benchmarking** - Compare your metrics to industry averages
- **Predictive alerts** - Get warned about potential issues before they escalate

### AI Response Suggestions

Writing review responses can be time-consuming. Our new AI response generator will:

- Analyze the review content and sentiment
- Generate personalized response suggestions
- Maintain your brand voice and tone
- Learn from your editing patterns

## Q2: Integration Expansion

### New CRM Integrations

We're adding native integrations with:

- **HubSpot** - Bi-directional sync with contact properties
- **Salesforce** - Survey triggers from opportunity stages
- **Encompass** - Deep LOS integration for automatic triggers

### Microsoft Teams Integration

Get real-time notifications and respond to reviews directly from Teams:

- Channel notifications for new reviews
- Weekly digest summaries
- Leaderboard tabs for team motivation

## Q3: Mobile App Launch

### RepWell Mobile

Manage your reviews on the go with our new mobile app:

- View dashboard metrics at a glance
- Read and respond to new reviews
- Send survey requests from your phone
- Push notifications for important alerts

### Contact Picker Integration

Quickly send surveys by selecting contacts from your phone:

- SMS survey option
- Quick send from recent contacts
- Sync with your existing contact lists

## Q4: Advanced Features

### Testimonial Generator

Transform your best reviews into marketing assets:

- AI-powered quote extraction
- Social media-ready formats
- Image generation for testimonial graphics
- Easy export and sharing

### Social Media Auto-Publish

Automatically share your best reviews on social media:

- Connect Facebook, LinkedIn, and Twitter
- Customizable post templates
- Scheduling options
- Performance tracking

Your feedback drives our roadmap. Here's how we decide what to build:
